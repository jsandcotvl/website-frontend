About Us

Built on Trust. Driven by Quality.

JS & CO was founded on a simple belief: clients deserve a partner who says what they mean, quotes what they deliver, and delivers what they quote. Under the proprietorship of Mohamed Rafeek, we have grown into a multi-service construction and engineering company serving families, businesses and institutions.

We don't chase every project — we focus on the ones we can do well. Whether it is a home build, a steel shade structure, an interior fit-out or a full cost consultation, our promise stays the same: honest advice, transparent pricing, practical engineering and workmanship we are happy to put our name on.

Customer satisfaction is not a slogan for us. It is the reason most of our work comes through referrals, and it is the standard we hold ourselves to on every single site.

  • Quality-focused execution on every project
  • Transparent quotations and billing
  • Practical, cost-aware engineering solutions
  • Direct communication with the proprietor
